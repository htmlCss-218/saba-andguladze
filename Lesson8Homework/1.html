Origin

Whales are a widely distributed and diverse group of fully aquatic placental marine mammals. They belong to the infraorder Cetacea, excluding dolphins and porpoises. As mammals, whales share the defining traits of air-breathing, warm-bloodedness, giving live birth, nursing their young on milk, and having hair.
