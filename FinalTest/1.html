Our Brand Story

Origins: Our company started in 2010, when we noticed a lack of eco-friendly fashion options in the market. We decided to create a brand that blends sustainability with high-quality designs to offer the best eco-conscious fashion for all.

Mission & Vision: Our mission is simple: to provide high-quality, sustainable clothing for all sizes. We believe that everyone should have access to fashion that is not only stylish but also kind to the planet. Our vision is to become a global leader in eco-conscious fashion, setting new standards for ethical production.

Founders: Founded by Saba andghuladze, our team is driven by a passion for creating positive change. With years of experience in the fashion industry, we are committed to building a brand that makes a real difference.
